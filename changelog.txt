1.0.0:
Highlights & Architecture:
    - Independent Versioning & Multi-Client: The addon version is now decoupled from the WoW patch number, using a single package covering both modern Retail and WoW Forever (build 1.60.1).
    - Settings Redesign: Options are reorganized into dedicated pages (Uber UI, Unit Frames, Raid & Group Frames, Arena, Nameplates, Other UI). Most settings now apply live without requiring a UI reload, and /uberui works alongside /uui.
    - Performance & Taint Hardening: Combat-safe deferred frame updates, eliminated maxHealth taint crashes on WoW Forever, deduplicated texture settings code, and converted optional hooks to load only on demand.
    - LibSharedMedia Integration: Bar texture dropdowns now support all third-party SharedMedia textures, and Uber UI's internal textures are shared outward as "Uber UI ".

Auras & Buffs/Debuffs:
    - Granular Aura Styling: Replaced the single combined dropdown with per-frame controls for Zoom, Buff Border, Debuff Border, Border Shape (Rounded/Square), Thickness, and Position across all unit, arena, and raid frames.
    - New All Auras Tool: Main settings page now includes one-shot actions to quickly apply zoom, border, shape, thickness, or position across all aura locations at once.
    - Square Aura Borders: Added flat square aura borders (1px default) with inside/outside placement that preserve dispel colors and purge highlights cleanly in combat without taint.
    - Spacing & Overlap Fixes: Target and Focus auras match Blizzard's native sizes/spacing, eliminated combat buff/debuff row overlap, added Target of Target row width toggles, and replaced purgeable buff glows with clean, thin white borders.

Nameplates & Arena:
    - Custom Nameplate Auras: Optional native aura engine supporting zoom, custom borders, cooldown timers with expiring threshold colors, and pandemic refresh highlights.
    - Square Nameplates: Added flat square health bar borders (1-4px) with matching selection outlines, defaulting to the "Blizzard_Flat" texture.
    - Arena Enhancements & Fixes: Shows clean 1/2/3 markers over enemy nameplates in arenas, fixed selection glow toggle restoration, and resolved WoW Forever cast bar spacing.

Unit Frames, Cooldown Manager & Other UI:
    - Expanded Theme Darkening: Darkening, status bar textures, and health coloring extended to bag slots, action bar dividers, damage meter frames, and third-party minimap buttons. Heal prediction and absorb colors are preserved on custom textures.
    - Cooldown Manager Upgrades: Added square zoomed icons (1-8px borders), tracked debuff dispel colors, and optional pandemic refresh highlights (glow, border, or marching ants) that stay aligned with Edit Mode icon scaling.
    - WoW Forever Swing Timers: Added darkening, separate bar textures, fill color pickers, label shadow toggles, and square border options (1-4px) for main hand, off hand, and ranged swing bars.
