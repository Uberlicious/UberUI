1.0.0:
    - First release under the new versioning scheme: the addon version is now independent of the WoW patch number, since one package now covers both retail and WoW Forever. See the Interface line for client compatibility instead.
    - Aura styling (Zoom / Dark Border / Both / None, with real dispel-type-colored borders) is now available on Player, Target, Focus, Party, Compact Raid/Party (including private boss debuffs and the Big Defensive icon), Arena (Crowd Control and Diminishing Returns trackers), and Boss frames.
    - Frame darkening, bar texture customization, and health bar coloring extended to cover all of the above frame types, plus bag slots, action bar dividers, and the damage meter.
    - Hide Honor (hides the PvP badge/icon) now works correctly on Player, Target, Focus, and Party frames, on both retail and WoW Forever.
    - Fixed UI taint and errors during combat by deferring frame updates until combat ends (arena, party, player, target, and nameplate frames).
    - Compatibility pass for the WoW Forever client (build 1.60.1) alongside retail.
