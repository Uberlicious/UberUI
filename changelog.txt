1.0.4:
    - Note: there is no 1.0.3 -- that version number was already used by a 2018 release of Uber UI, so this build skips to 1.0.4.
    - Cursor Ring (Other UI): A ring that follows your mouse cursor, in your class color or a color of your choice, Normal or Thin, adjustable size, optionally only in combat. It stays on screen while the cursor is hidden (holding a mouse button to turn the camera). Comes with a live preview.
    - GCD Ring: An optional ring just inside the cursor ring that sweeps away as your global cooldown runs, with its own style and color (white by default). Works on WoW Forever too.
    - Cleaner Settings: Setting names no longer repeat the section they're in (e.g. "Zoom Icons" instead of "Target Zoom Icons"). Your saved settings are unchanged.

1.0.2:
    - Player Aura Timers: Fixed timers going invisible when Duration Inside Icon is on or the duration text size is changed.
    - Pixel Glow: Now animates on real nameplate auras (it froze or vanished there) and no longer stops when you debuff another target. It defaults to Inside, and the nameplate Pixel Glow Position option now unlocks when Pixel Glow is picked.

1.0.1:
    - Friendly Name Health (Nameplates): See friendly players' health at a glance on name-only nameplates in the open world. Pick an Underline (under or above the name), a Dot (left, right, above or below), or tint the Name itself from red to green (class color at full health). Adjustable underline thickness, dot size and offsets, optional hide-at-full-health, and pixel-perfect drawing that stays crisp as nameplates scale.
    - Live Settings Previews: Aura sections, the Cooldown Manager (icons and Tracked Bars), nameplate health bars, nameplate pandemic highlights and Friendly Name Health each show a live preview that updates as you change settings, at the size things really appear on screen (including WoW Forever's nameplate level box).
    - Aura Text Options: Every aura location except Arena can resize its duration text and stack count and move the stack count to any corner or edge with fine offsets. Player auras can show their timer inside the icon, outlined, with tighter rows. Defaults stay true to Blizzard's own layout, and counts and timers always draw above borders.
    - Pixel Glow Highlight: New marching-dashes pandemic highlight for Cooldown Manager icons, Tracked Bars and nameplate auras, with inside/outside placement. It replaces the stretched Marching Ants on Tracked Bars. Glows now sit over a black border that hides any debuff color underneath.
    - Tracked Bars & Duration Text: Cooldown Manager Tracked Bars gain texture, square shape, darkening and their own pandemic styles (including Bar Fill Color). Target, Focus and Compact Raid/Party auras can show duration text, and enemy nameplates get buff limits with important and purgeable filters.
    - Fixes: Rounded aura borders no longer slip under icons, zoomed Player aura borders match their dispel-colored ones, Player timers sit just under the border, compact raid stack counts are sized properly, and WoW Forever is now detected correctly (its Forever-only styling like copper square borders and the nameplate level box applies). Swing timer bar color is now one None / Custom / Class choice.

1.0.0:
Highlights & Architecture:
    - Independent Versioning & Multi-Client: The addon version is now decoupled from the WoW patch number, using a single package covering both modern Retail and WoW Forever (build 1.60.1).
    - Settings Redesign: Options are reorganized into dedicated pages (Uber UI, Unit Frames, Raid & Group Frames, Arena, Nameplates, Other UI). Most settings now apply live without requiring a UI reload, and /uberui works alongside /uui.
    - Performance & Taint Hardening: Combat-safe deferred frame updates, eliminated maxHealth taint crashes on WoW Forever, deduplicated texture settings code, and converted optional hooks to load only on demand.
    - LibSharedMedia Integration: Bar texture dropdowns now support all third-party SharedMedia textures, and Uber UI's internal textures are shared outward as "Uber UI ".

Auras & Buffs/Debuffs:
    - Granular Aura Styling: Replaced the single combined dropdown with per-frame controls for Zoom, Buff Border, Debuff Border, Border Shape (Rounded/Square), Thickness, and Position across all unit, arena, and raid frames.
    - New All Auras Tool: Main settings page now includes one-shot actions to quickly apply zoom, border, shape, thickness, or position across all aura locations at once.
    - Square Aura Borders: Added flat square aura borders (1px default) with inside/outside placement that preserve dispel colors and purge highlights cleanly in combat without taint.
    - Spacing & Overlap Fixes: Target and Focus auras match Blizzard's native sizes/spacing, eliminated combat buff/debuff row overlap, added Target of Target row width toggles, and replaced purgeable buff glows with clean, thin white borders.

Nameplates & Arena:
    - Custom Nameplate Auras: Optional native aura engine supporting zoom, custom borders, cooldown timers with expiring threshold colors, and pandemic refresh highlights.
    - Square Nameplates: Added flat square health bar borders (1-4px) with matching selection outlines, defaulting to the "Blizzard_Flat" texture.
    - Arena Enhancements & Fixes: Shows clean 1/2/3 markers over enemy nameplates in arenas, fixed selection glow toggle restoration, and resolved WoW Forever cast bar spacing.

Unit Frames, Cooldown Manager & Other UI:
    - Expanded Theme Darkening: Darkening, status bar textures, and health coloring extended to bag slots, action bar dividers, damage meter frames, and third-party minimap buttons. Heal prediction and absorb colors are preserved on custom textures.
    - Cooldown Manager Upgrades: Added square zoomed icons (1-8px borders), tracked debuff dispel colors, and optional pandemic refresh highlights (glow, border, or marching ants) that stay aligned with Edit Mode icon scaling.
    - WoW Forever Swing Timers: Added darkening, separate bar textures, fill color pickers, label shadow toggles, and square border options (1-4px) for main hand, off hand, and ranged swing bars.
